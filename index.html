Questions

Which pairs are covered?

Exactly six: EUR/USD, USD/JPY, USD/CHF, USD/CAD, CAD/USD and NZD/USD. Crosses like EUR/GBP or GBP/JPY are not part of the system and never appear in alerts.

What does a round alert look like?

One block per pair: direction, current price, trigger line, entry zone, status (not confirmed / watch / confirmed) and distance in pips — followed by a final decision with next scan and next confirmation times.

Do you guarantee profits?

No. The service provides technical analysis and risk-engineered levels only. Markets involve real risk, and past or projected outcomes are not a promise of future results. Trade at your own risk.

How do I cancel?

Open the channel in Telegram, tap the subscription status, and cancel. Your access ends at the current billing period.

Why is there nothing on weekends?

FX closes Friday evening and reopens Monday. The system deliberately produces no signals over the weekend, so the first meaningful check is Monday's daily close.
